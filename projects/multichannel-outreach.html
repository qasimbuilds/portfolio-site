Execution Architecture

Research → Personalize → Outreach → Detect → Route → Sync

The system is designed around event and condition logic rather than a single linear message sequence, allowing different channels and outcomes to be handled without losing the lead state.

1

1 · Lead intake

Bring lead records into the workflow with the required contact, company and outreach fields.

2

2 · AI personalization

Generate context-aware outreach content from the available lead and company information before sending.

3

3 · Initial email

Send the first outreach message and store the relevant status so later steps know where the lead is in the sequence.

4

4 · Follow-up logic

Wait for the configured interval, check the current lead state and continue only when the stop conditions have not been met.

5

5 · Channel fallback

If the next preferred channel is unavailable, use the configured fallback path such as LinkedIn or WhatsApp rather than silently dropping the lead.

6

6 · Reply detection

Detect replies or positive engagement and stop unnecessary follow-up when the lead has responded.

7

7 · CRM synchronization

Update outreach status, last activity and relevant lead state so the automation layer and CRM remain aligned.

8

8 · Error handling

Keep integration failures and unexpected states visible so a failed API or channel action does not disappear without trace.
